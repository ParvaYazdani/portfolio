Project Management

Alongside engineering work, I carry sub-projects across departments, initiate project ideas, and lead consortia through to successful funding submission, including supervising students along the way.

Project Acquisition & Consortium Leadership

Context
Two proposals: VANSIM (the Virtual Automation Network Simulation concept, originating from the 5GiwCow project) and ConvAssist (a new AI engineering concept).
Outcome
Both funding proposals were fully developed and submitted, each with a complete project consortium. I developed the basic idea for both concepts together with another team member and also led the VANSIM consortium.

Sub-Project Management

Context
In academic research projects, researchers usually manage the parts they are responsible for themselves. In 6G Platform I led the innovation-management working group, coordinating the whitepaper together with the working group members and external project partners through regular meetings, distributing the tasks among the participants, and planning the next steps.
Outcome
Contributions were delivered in time for the project's publications and reports and led to the expected results of the corresponding project.

Student Supervision

Context
Two students: one bachelor's thesis ("A Communication Control Co-Design Approach in Industrial Automation"), and a student research assistant who supported the first experiments on the VANSIM idea.
